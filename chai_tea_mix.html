Chai Tea Mix

Combine ginger, cinnamon, cardamom, clove, instant tea, and powdered milk for a quick chai mix. Home cooks praise this gift-worthy chai mix for its budget-friendly, customizable ingredients. "This is my go-to Chai Tea recipe ... Love, love, love this recipe!!" —Allrecipes member Mary.

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups white sugar
  • 1 ½ cups unsweetened instant tea
  • 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1 cup powdered non-dairy creamer
  • 1 cup French vanilla flavored powdered non-dairy creamer
  • 2 teaspoons ground ginger
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
